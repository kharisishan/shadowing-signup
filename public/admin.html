Advanced: paste a list or replace everything

Paste a list instead

One line per campus, in this order: Campus | dates | times | roles. Separate dates and times with commas, and roles with semicolons. Every date, time and role is combined, so the line below makes 2 dates × 1 time × 4 roles = 8 slots.

Add keeps everything that is already there and only adds new slots. Replace makes the list exactly what you typed: slots that are not in it are removed and their sign-ups cancelled. Use Copy current sessions below to start from what is live. Write a note in brackets after a date if you like, such as 21st October (GPN). The year is worked out for you and shown in the preview.
